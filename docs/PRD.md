# PRD — Lexora Law Associates (Demo Website)

## 1. What to Build
A demonstration marketing website for a law firm ("Lexora Law Associates"), built in React + Tailwind CSS. Goal: show a polished, trustworthy, conversion-focused site whose main action is **Book a Free Consultation**.

Design direction (from the UI styles list provided by the client, not the Chiku Cab brand):
- UI styles: **Minimalism** (white space, clean typography, focus on content) + **Bento Grid** (practice areas, stats, features in modular cards)
- Palette suited to a law firm: deep navy, warm gold accent, off-white background
- Typography: serif display headings (authority) with a clean sans body
- Full-width layouts, generous spacing, soft rounded cards with thin borders

## 2. Targeted User
- Individuals facing family, property, criminal, or consumer disputes who need a lawyer quickly
- Small business owners needing contract, GST/tax, or corporate counsel
- Mobile-first visitors in India who prefer WhatsApp / call over long forms

Needs: instant trust (credentials, results), clear practice areas, simple way to contact, fast load.

## 3. Features
- Sticky navbar with Call and WhatsApp buttons
- Hero with headline and consultation CTA
- Trust stats strip (years, cases, clients)
- Bento grid of practice areas
- Attorney profiles
- Process steps (how we work)
- Client testimonials (demo content)
- FAQ accordion
- Consultation request form with validation (demo, no backend)
- Floating WhatsApp button, footer with office details
- Pages: Home, Practice Areas, Attorneys, Contact

Note: all names, numbers, and testimonials are placeholder demo content.
